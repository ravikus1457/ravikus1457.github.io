      How to play

      Crapless craps in a minute

      On the come-out roll, a 7 wins on the pass line and the line can’t lose. There are no craps: every other total, including 2, 3, 11 and 12, becomes the point. The shooter then rolls until the point repeats, which wins, or a 7 rolls, which is a seven-out.

      Making bets

      Pick a chip, then tap a spot on the layout. Tap again to add more. Right-click or long-press a bet to take it down, or switch on Remove and tap it.

      Each number has a Buy strip above it and a Place strip below it. Come bets travel into the number box, and tapping that box adds odds to the come bet sitting there. The pass line can’t come down once a point is set, and a come bet can’t come down once it has traveled to a number.

      Low rolls, High rolls and Roll ’em all open at the start of a hand. They win when every number in the set rolls before a 7, and the layout lights up each number as it hits.

      Table buttons

      Undo Z
      Takes back the last chip you placed since the last roll.
      Clear
      Takes down every bet that’s allowed to come down.
      Repeat R
      Puts last roll’s bets back up.
      Across A
      Places every number except the point, sized to pay without rounding.
      Come-out bets
      Off by default. When working, place, buy, hardway and come odds win or lose on the come-out roll too.
      Bets off
      Calls your place, buy, hardway and come odds off on every roll until you turn them back on. They stay on the table.

      Payouts

      By default the table pays whole dollars and keeps the change, like a casino. Hover over or focus any spot to see its payout and the bet size that avoids rounding. You can switch to exact cents in table settings.

      About

      Mezware Crapless Craps is a free practice table for crapless craps, also called never-ever craps. It uses play money only.

      The dice are thrown with real-time physics and read from whichever face lands on top. Every bet pays by the odds printed on the layout, and the statistics panel compares your rolls with the expected distribution.

      Your bankroll, bets and history are saved in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off.
